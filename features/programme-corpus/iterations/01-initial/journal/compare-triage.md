# Compare triage — every discrepancy, read and judged

> The five `--compare` runs (seed vs independent layer-2 re-read) produce 233
> discrepancy lines. Until now only the counts were looked at. This file reads
> every line and files it under exactly one verdict, with the evidence.
>
> Sources: `project/docs/reference/curriculum/tadarroj-3as-*-2022.pdf`, rendered
> with `pdftoppm` at 150 dpi to locate and 300–1200 dpi to prove. `pdftotext` was
> used ONLY for glyph identity (diacritic codepoints, stray punctuation presence)
> — never for content or totals, per the standing disqualification.
> Nothing was modified: no seed file, no l2 file, no product code.

## Verdicts

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| **artifact** | A known structural difference between how the seed and an l2 file record the same page fact. No content differs. Three classes: **window** (anchors are ~6-word first-words windows; seed stores maths as LaTeX where l2 stops short or types raw glyphs; edge punctuation and bullet markers tokenize differently — the seed encodes printed bullet dashes as array structure, l2 transcribes them), **unitLabel** (seed carries the summary table's `nameText`; l2 records the per-week محور cell; the source itself spells them differently — preserved deliberately), and **attribution** (a merged cell physically spans several rows or a week border; seed pinned it to one row, l2 to another; both are readings of one cell and the text exists once in the seed). |
| **l2-wrong** | The seed matches the page; the independent re-read does not. The l2 files are NOT fixed — their independence is the only reason comparing is worth anything. |
| **seed-wrong** | The page disagrees with the seed. Proven at 300–1200 dpi. |
| **unresolved** | Could not be settled from the page. |

## The three seed defects (page-proven)

1. **techmath week 25 row[2].competencies — `السُلَّمي` stored, page prints `السُلمي`.**
   The lam is BARE on the page: no shadda, no fatha (1200 dpi crop; text layer has
   DAMMA only). Every sibling instance in all three documents prints `السُلَّمي`
   (fatha+shadda) — this one instance does not. The seed normalized it to match its
   siblings; the l2 (`السُلّمي`) is wrong about it too. This is exactly the
   "shadda placement varies by instance" trap already on record.

2. **sciences week 6 row[1].contents — printed stray leading period dropped.**
   The page prints `. النهايات باستعمال المبرهنات…` — a stray period at the visual
   start of the cell (600 dpi crop, p8; text layer `.النهايات`; almost certainly a
   bidi-misplaced sentence period in the source document). The corpus policy is to
   preserve stray punctuation (`؟`, `أو.`, `الأصغر:.` all kept); the seed dropped
   this one. The l2 recorded it. Cosmetic, but a fidelity defect by the corpus's
   own standard. Note the techmath twin (`week 6 · row[3].contents`) has the SAME
   printed leading period and there the seed kept it and the l2 dropped it.

3. **sciences week 8 row[1].contents — same cell, repeated week (تابع), same drop.**
   Page 9 prints the same `. النهايات…` leading period; text layer confirms both
   occurrences.

## Notable l2-wrong findings (the seed survived independent fire)

- `نُدرج` (math w13): page prints NOON at 1200 dpi, text layer NOON+DAMMA — l2 read تُدرج.
- `السُلَّمي` ×14 (math 5, sciences 5, techmath 4): the l2 uniformly typed `السُلّمي`; the page prints the fatha on every one of those instances.
- `ومستو` ×3 (math w26, techmath w26 ×2): l2 added a tanwin the page does not print.
- `أنّ` (techmath w15): shadda printed; l2 dropped it.
- `لهده` (gestion w15): the preserved source typo is on the page; l2 normalized to لهذه.
- `نذكّر` (gestion w7): shadda only; l2 added a fatha.
- `وتطبيقات له` (sciences w25): l2 read وتطبيقاته; the page doesn't print it.
- gestion w23: l2 shifted `إنشاء مستقيم تعديل خطي. (تابع)` one column (into competencies); the border-in-frame crop shows it in contents, where the seed has it.
- lettres w14: l2 misjoined a line wrap (`وتمثيلها بيانيا`); the page breaks `وتمثيلها / حل المعادلات بيانيا` and the seed preserves the printed order.

## Observation outside the discrepancy list (agreement is not proof)

Math w25 row[0] competencies ends `…لتعيين معادلة لمستوٍ.` in BOTH seed and l2 — so
compare flags nothing — but the 600/1200 dpi crops appear to print `لمستو` with no
tanwin and no trailing period. Both readers may share a normalization there. Worth a
follow-up pass on tanwin-final words that both layers agree on; the anchor diff can
never surface it.

## Verdict of the whole exercise

The corpus is MORE trustworthy than the counts suggested — but not for free.
233 discrepancies reduced to: 204 structural artifacts of how two honest readings
encode one page, 26 places where the seed beat an independent reader, and 3 real
seed defects, all three at the finest glyph level (one absent shadda, one stray
period twice). No content-level defect — no wrong week, no wrong cell text, no
misattributed paragraph — survived triage anywhere in the five documents. The
counts hid both the good news (the seeds' diacritic-level fidelity is better than
the independent re-read's) and the bad (three glyph-level defects sat unread in
the list, same class as the two already found). A count is not evidence; the list
was.
